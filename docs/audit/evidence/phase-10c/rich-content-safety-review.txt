Phase 10C rich-content safety review

Source facts:
- post_translations.content is a longText field authored through Filament RichEditor.
- No project-approved public HTML sanitizer package or verified RichEditor public renderer is present.
- Phase 10C intentionally avoids raw `{!! $content !!}` article-body rendering.

Implementation:
- App\Services\PublicSite\BlogContent::derivePlainText() decodes entities, removes script/style/iframe/object/embed/noscript blocks, strips remaining tags, normalizes whitespace, and returns plain text.
- App\Services\PublicSite\BlogContent::plainTextParagraphs() prepares escaped plain-text paragraphs for Blade.
- resources/views/public/blog/show.blade.php renders article paragraphs through escaped Blade output.
- SEO fields `seo_title` and `seo_description` are not rendered as visible content.

Test evidence:
- Tests\Feature\Public\PublicBlogTest::test_rich_editor_content_is_rendered_as_safe_plain_text_without_raw_markup asserts visible safe body text and rejects CMS-injected script markup, event handlers, raw formatting tags, and visible SEO field leakage.

Result:
PASS. RichEditor content is rendered as an escaped/plain-text fallback and is not blindly emitted as raw HTML.
