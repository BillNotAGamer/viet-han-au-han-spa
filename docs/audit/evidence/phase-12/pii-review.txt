1. ZERO PII POLICY MANDATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Viet Han Au Han Spa strictly forbids the transmission of Personally Identifiable
Information (PII) to third-party marketing tags (Google Tag Manager, Google
Analytics 4, Meta Pixel) via client-side JavaScript or dataLayer events.

PII Attributes Strictly Excluded from Client Payload:
- customer_name (Customer Name)
- phone (Raw Phone Number)
- phone_normalized (Normalized E.164 Phone)
- email (Customer Email Address)
- customer_note / notes (Free-form Notes / Inquiries)
- booking reference (Internal Reference ID)
