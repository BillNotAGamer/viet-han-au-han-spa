Phase 10D SiteSettings public-safety review

Contact settings source:
- App\Services\PublicSite\StaticPageContent::contactSettings()
- resources/views/components/public/footer.blade.php

Explicit Contact allow-list:
- contact.phone
- contact.email
- contact.address
- business.hours
- social.facebook_url
- social.zalo_url
- social.youtube_url

Public access API:
- StaticPageContent uses SiteSettings::getPublic(...) for each allow-listed key.
- Footer uses SiteSettings::getPublic(...) for supported contact footer fields.
- Contact view receives prepared arrays and does not call SiteSettings directly.

Private setting boundary:
- The Contact page does not call SiteSettings::publicSettings().
- The Contact page does not dump all public settings.
- Private settings are not returned by getPublic(...).
- Unrelated public settings are not included because only the allow-listed keys are requested.

Contact URL safety:
- Email becomes mailto: only after FILTER_VALIDATE_EMAIL.
- Phone becomes tel: only after normalization to + and digits and a conservative numeric pattern.
- External/social URLs become active links only when parse_url reports http or https.
- javascript:, data:, file:, and missing schemes are omitted.

Verdict:
PASS. Contact information is prepared through an explicit public-safe allow-list and unsafe active links are omitted.
